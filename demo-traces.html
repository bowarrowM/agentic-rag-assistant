The answer is also self-contradictory. The groundedness check flagged it UNSUPPORTED. The verdict is currently advisory: it is recorded in the trace, it does not block the response. The judge is the same 3B model that produced the answer, and its false-negative rate has not been measured — so promoting it to a hard gate would risk blocking correct answers. Building that measurement is the prerequisite for gating.
Retrieval failure and generation failure are separated deliberately: if the retrieved chunks are wrong, the fix is in retrieval; if the chunks are right and the answer is still wrong, the fix is in generation. The trace is what makes that distinction visible.
