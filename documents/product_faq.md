# Kitty Pay — Frequently Asked Questions

## Accounts

**What is Kitty Pay?**
Kitty Pay is a digital wallet. You can hold a balance, send and receive money, link a debit or credit card to top up, and pay Kitty-accepting merchants.

**How do I open an account?**
Download the app and sign up with your email and phone number. Basic accounts are verified with an email and phone confirmation. To raise your limits you complete identity verification (a photo ID and a selfie check) under Settings → Verification.

**Is there a fee to open or hold an account?**
No. Opening and holding a Kitty Pay wallet is free. Fees only apply to specific actions, listed under "Fees" below.

## Sending & Receiving Money

**How do I send money?**
Open the app, tap **Send**, choose a contact or enter their Kitty tag or phone number, enter the amount, and confirm. Transfers between Kitty Pay users are instant.

**Is sending money free?**
Sending money to another Kitty Pay user from your wallet balance is free. Sending money funded directly by a linked **credit** card carries a 1.5% funding fee (debit cards are free).

**Are there sending limits?**
Unverified accounts can send up to €500 per day. Fully verified accounts can send up to €10,000 per day and €50,000 per month.

**Can I cancel a transfer I already sent?**
Transfers to other Kitty Pay users are instant and cannot be reversed once accepted. If you sent money to the wrong person, open a dispute so an agent can help recover it.

## Cards & Top-ups

**How do I add money to my wallet?**
Link a debit or credit card under Settings → Cards, then tap **Top up**. Debit-card top-ups are free; credit-card top-ups carry a 1.5% fee. Top-ups usually clear instantly, but bank processing can take up to 30 minutes.

**Which cards are supported?**
Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards issued in supported countries. Prepaid cards are not supported.

## Fees

- Holding a wallet: **free**
- Sending from wallet balance or debit card: **free**
- Sending or topping up funded by a **credit card**: **1.5%**
- Currency conversion (spending in another currency): **0.9%** on top of the exchange rate
- Withdrawing your balance to a linked bank account: **free**, arrives in 1–3 business days

## Refunds

**Do you offer refunds?**
Refunds apply to payments made to merchants, not to peer-to-peer transfers. If a merchant approves a refund, the money is returned to your Kitty Pay balance within 5 to 10 business days.

**A merchant won't refund me — what can I do?**
If you can't resolve it with the merchant, open a dispute and a Kitty Pay agent will review the transaction.

## Security

**How is my money protected?**
Balances are held with a regulated partner bank. The app uses AES-256 encryption at rest and requires biometric or PIN confirmation for every payment.

**Do you support two-factor authentication?**
Yes. 2FA via an authenticator app or SMS is available to all users and required for adding a new card or changing your linked bank account. Enable it under Settings → Security.

**What happens if I see a payment I didn't make?**
Freeze your wallet immediately from Settings → Security → Freeze, then open a dispute so an agent can investigate the unauthorized activity.
