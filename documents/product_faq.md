# Nimbus — Frequently Asked Questions

## Accounts & Billing

**How much does Nimbus cost?**
Nimbus has three plans: Free (up to 3 projects), Pro ($12/user/month), and Business ($25/user/month). Annual billing saves 20%.

**Can I change plans later?**
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from Settings → Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

**Do you offer refunds?**
We offer a full refund within 14 days of any payment, no questions asked. Contact support@nimbus.example to request one.

## Projects & Tasks

**Is there a limit on tasks per project?**
No. All paid plans have unlimited tasks. The Free plan is limited only by the 3-project cap.

**Can I archive a project instead of deleting it?**
Yes. Archiving hides a project from your active list but keeps all its data. Archived projects don't count toward your plan's project limit.

## Security

**Where is my data stored?**
All data is stored in EU data centers (Frankfurt) and encrypted at rest with AES-256.

**Do you support two-factor authentication?**
Yes. 2FA via authenticator app is available on all plans, including Free. Enable it in Settings → Security.
