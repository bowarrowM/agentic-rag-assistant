# Kitty Pay — Support Policies

## Response Times

- **In-app support assistant:** available 24/7 for account, payment, and security questions.
- **Human agent (via ticket):** first response within 24 hours for general issues, and within 4 hours for security or unauthorized-payment reports.

## Refund Policy

Refunds apply to merchant payments, not to peer-to-peer transfers between Kitty Pay users. When a merchant approves a refund, the funds are returned to your Kitty Pay balance within 5 to 10 business days. Kitty Pay cannot force a merchant to issue a refund, but if you cannot resolve it directly, you can open a dispute for an agent to review.

## Disputes & Unauthorized Payments

If you see a payment you did not authorize, or a transfer sent to the wrong recipient:
1. Freeze your wallet from Settings → Security → Freeze.
2. Open a dispute so a Kitty Pay agent can investigate.

Disputes are acknowledged within 24 hours and most are resolved within 10 business days. For confirmed unauthorized transactions, eligible funds are returned to your balance once the investigation completes.

## Account Freezes

A wallet may be frozen automatically if our systems detect unusual activity, or manually by you for safety. While frozen, you cannot send money or top up, but you can still receive funds. To unfreeze, complete the security check under Settings → Security, or open a ticket if the freeze was not initiated by you.

## Data Export & Deletion

You own your data. You can export your full transaction history as CSV or PDF at any time from Settings → Export. If you close your account, your personal data is deleted within 30 days in line with GDPR, except records Kitty Pay must retain for financial-regulation and anti-fraud purposes.

## Service Availability

Kitty Pay targets 99.9% uptime. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance and performed during low-traffic weekends. Status and incident history are published at status.kittypay.example.
