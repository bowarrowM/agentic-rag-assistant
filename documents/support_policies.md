# Nimbus — Support Policies

## Response Times

Support response times depend on your plan:
- **Free plan:** community forum only, no guaranteed response time.
- **Pro plan:** email support, first response within 24 business hours.
- **Business plan:** priority email support, first response within 4 business hours.

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of any charge. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Partial refunds are not offered for mid-cycle downgrades; instead, unused time is credited to your account.

## Data Export & Deletion

You own your data. You can export all projects and tasks as CSV or JSON at any time from Settings → Export. If you close your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days, in line with GDPR requirements.

## Service Availability

Nimbus targets 99.9% uptime. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance and performed during low-traffic weekends. Status and incident history are published at status.nimbus.example.
